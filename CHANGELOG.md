## 0.1.0 — 2026-09-01

### Added

- Initial local-first deterministic engine foundation.
- Typed semantic atoms, relations, protected fields, delivery modes, and controlled literal forms.
- Explicit register context with domain, surface, mode, stakes, safety class, audience, focalizer, channel, and execution bounds.
- Bounded declarative profile compilation and predicate evaluation.
- Fixed priority tiers from safety through ornament.
- Operative, experiential, and interpretive content layers.
- Standard and accessibility-equivalent representations.
- Deterministic coverage, accessibility, technical-claim, cadence, exposition, dialogue, focalization, embodiment, depth, action, externality, and scene-ending validators.
- Canonical serialization and SHA-256 content digests.
- Relational Systems Register definition and conformance expectations.
- Typed failure codes for contract, semantic, accessibility, profile, context, candidate, bound, and evidence failures.
- Candidate-addressable derivation receipts with closed topology and full public-result binding.
- Deterministic literal fallback labeling when no mapped realization is available.
- Seventy-one adversarial tests covering semantic drift, safety and accessibility invariants, hostile data, deterministic replay, receipt integrity, and trust-boundary claims.

### Security and integrity

- Profiles are restricted to bounded plain data and registered validators.
- Protected engine priorities cannot be redefined by profiles.
- Runtime design excludes network access, telemetry, dynamic execution, and ambient randomness.
- Evidence trust is explicitly limited to reproducibility and conformance rather than truth or authenticity.
- Safety-critical realization fails closed outside the engine's supported English lexical boundary.
